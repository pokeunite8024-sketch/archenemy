# Archenemy

Category: Forensics  |  Worth up to 300 points

## The challenge
John likes Arch Linux. (He will tell you so.) Today he sent over a picture and insisted there is nothing in it.

He also left his favourite words lying around in wordlist.txt. What is he hiding?

## Files in this folder
- `arched.png`
- `wordlist.txt`

## Flag format
Flags look like `ARGHYA{some_words_here}`. Submit the whole flag, including `ARGHYA{` and `}`,
on the scoring platform. Flags for this challenge are case-insensitive.
